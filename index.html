About Us

Your neighbourhood hospital on the Pune–Nagar Road

Chaitanya Hospital serves families in Koregaon Bhima and the surrounding villages, so that good medical care does not mean a long trip into Pune. Come in for a check-up, an illness, an injury or ongoing treatment, and our doctors and staff will look after you.

  • Open 7 days

    Morning and evening OPD every day, including Sunday.

  • Easy to reach

    On the Nagar Road at Koregaon Bhima, near Sri Datta Tyres.

  • Clear explanations

    Your illness and treatment explained in simple words, in Marathi, Hindi or English.

  • Care for the whole family

    Children, adults and elders, all at one place.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Where is Chaitanya Hospital?

Central Warehouse Koregaon Bhima, off Nagar Road, near Sri Datta Tyres, Pune, Maharashtra 412216. It is easy to reach from Sanaswadi, Shikrapur, Wagholi, Lonikand and Perne.

What are the OPD timings?

All days of the week, 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM and 5:00 PM – 9:00 PM. The hospital is open on Sundays too.

How do I book an appointment?

Call +91 75070 77608, or fill in the appointment form on this page and send it on WhatsApp. The hospital will confirm your time.

What should I bring to my visit?

Please bring your previous reports, prescriptions and the medicines you are currently taking.
